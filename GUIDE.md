# Basis passenger guide

## Plan without an account

1. Open the home page or **Plan a journey**.
2. Choose **From** and **To** from stop/place suggestions. Stops are listed first;
   route numbers help distinguish similarly named boarding points.
3. Optionally use your location. Permission is requested only after you choose it.
4. Select **Find a journey**. Compare changes, walking distance and ride distance.
5. Follow the boarding, intermediate-stop, transfer and alighting instructions.
   The map is optional; text instructions work without it.

The current local dataset is historical and for internal testing only. Check
coverage and source dates. Unknown fares and waiting times are not live estimates.
If no connection is found, choose another stop or increase endpoint walking up
to 2 km. Nearby stops do not imply a safe crossing between platforms.

## Share and save

**Share** warns that a link includes precise selected coordinates before copying.
Reloading or reopening the link restores both endpoints. Old links with only an
origin ask you to choose the destination.

**Save** keeps a favorite on this browser/device. **Saved** works without signing
in. Signing in optionally lets you synchronize selected favorites; importing
device favorites requires confirmation. Storage restrictions can prevent saving.

## Browse and report

**Routes** lists source-qualified lines and directional variants.
**Stops** lists boarding points and serving routes; **Find nearby stops** uses
one location request. Discovery distances are straight-line, not walking routes.

Use **Report an issue** on a route/stop or the contact form for support. Reports
are reviewed by staff; they do not automatically alter the published network.

## Staff

Authorized ADMIN/SUPER_ADMIN users open `/admin/network` to inspect import issues,
edit drafts, record verification/rights evidence, publish or restore dataset
versions, and review reports. See the
[operational runbook](docs/JOURNEY-PLANNER-RUNBOOK.md).

Legacy create/start/join/exit trip, ad-hoc location and transport-card workflows
have been retired. Old record links do not reveal archived passenger data.
